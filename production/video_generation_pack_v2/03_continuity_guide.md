# Continuity guide — what must survive every generated clip

Four clips will be cut together with approved stills, in one 77-second film. Any
drift in the list below is visible instantly, because the same face appears in the
opening fall, the rescue, the interior and the return.

---

## 1. The skier — locked identity

Source of truth: `references/characters/REF-CHAR-01-master-skier.png`

- Male, roughly 30–35, lean athletic build, not a bodybuilder.
- Short dark-blond / light-brown hair visible at the helmet edge; light stubble.
- Face: open, approachable, recognisable at medium distance. No scar, no beard change,
  no glasses, no earrings, no tattoos added.
- No celebrity resemblance. Do not "beautify" or de-age between clips.

## 2. Wardrobe — two approved states only

**Outdoors (Clips A, B, D):**

- Deep navy technical ski jacket, restrained orange zipper pulls / trim. No other colour block.
- Dark charcoal ski pants, black ski boots, dark insulated gloves.
- Matte black helmet; dark reflective goggles.
  - Clip A, start: goggles **over the eyes** (active skiing).
  - Clip A, after the fall + Clip B: goggles **pushed up on the helmet**, face readable.
  - Clip D: goggles **over the eyes** again.
- No logos, no badges, no sponsor marks, no readable text on garments.

**Indoors (Clip C):**

- Fitted dark navy technical training top with subtle orange accents.
- Dark charcoal training pants.
- Neutral indoor cross-training shoes — clearly not ski boots, not winter footwear.
- No watch changes, no added accessories.

**Rescue personnel (Clip B only):** dark red or navy technical shell jackets with
reflective strips, dark trousers, helmets, gloves, compact backpacks. Unbranded,
no text, no medical uniforms, no white coats.

## 3. Equipment

- Black skis with plausible modern sidecut; dark poles with round baskets.
- Skis never bend, warp, fuse, melt or pass through the body. Poles never curve.
- Boots stay in the bindings unless the action explicitly releases them
  (Clip A may release one ski; Clip B unfastens a boot deliberately).
- No branded or renamed hardware. Do not introduce a brand of ski, boot or helmet.

## 4. The center — visual identity

Source of truth: `REF-INT-02-master-rehabilitation-room.png`,
`REF-ARC-01-master-exterior.png`, `REF-SCENE-03`, `REF-SCENE-04`.

- Exterior: natural stone base, dark vertical timber slats, charcoal steel, generous
  glazing, low roofline, snowy terrain, Altai ridges behind. **No signage, no lettering,
  no logo on the building.** Brand copy is typeset in post only.
- Interior: dark timber slat walls, stone, polished concrete, panoramic windows,
  warm interior accents against cool daylight.
- Furnishings: existing plinth, bench, mats, resistance bands. **Do not add** monitors,
  diagnostic screens, treadmills, anti-gravity rigs, cryo chambers, pools, hoists,
  ski simulators — none is confirmed as the center's actual equipment.
- Two people indoors, maximum. No crowds, no queue, no staged team line-up.

## 5. Grade and light

- One restrained cinematic grade across the film: cool blue-grey outdoors, warm-neutral
  indoors, no orange-and-teal crush, no heavy vignette, no bloom.
- Snow reads white with detail — not clipped, not blue-cast to cyan.
- Outdoor light is flat/high overcast; a single soft brighter break on the ridge is
  acceptable in Clip D only.
- Interior light is warm practicals plus cool window fill; skin tones natural.
- Subtle organic grain everywhere. No HDR halos, no CGI gloss, no cartoon shading.

## 6. Action axis and geography

- Established in `REF-SCENE-01`: he travels **upper right → lower left**, downhill,
  camera on the **downhill front-quarter** side, on the same side of the action axis
  for the whole film.
- No clip may mirror the action. A horizontally flipped take is a reject.
- Same slope, same conifer edge, same ridge in A, B and D.

## 7. Physics and body rules (hard rejects)

Reject any take with: duplicated or extra limbs, extra heads, merged bodies, body
morphing between beats, feet sliding without weight, weightless falls, ragdoll flips,
impossible joint angles, walking or standing after a serious fall in Clip B, hands
passing through skis or snow, cloth clipping through the body.

## 8. Medical and factual restraint (non-negotiable)

- No blood, no visible wound, no deformity, no exposed bone, no dramatised agony.
- No diagnosis on screen or implied; no specific injury named; no timeline; no
  guaranteed outcome. Nothing may read as "he will definitely ski again".
- Rescue is generic mountain ski-patrol assistance: keep still, keep warm, move on a
  sled at walking pace. No ambulance, no helicopter, no hospital.
- Indoor work: supervised exercise, no hands-on manipulation of a specific injury.

## 9. Brand copy — post-production only

Never generated inside any clip. Typeset afterwards, exactly:

```
РЕАБИЛИТАЦИОННЫЙ ЦЕНТР АЛТАЙ
Твой сезон продолжается.
altaysportreab.ru
```

The decorative motif in `REF-SCENE-10` is not an official logo and must not be
recreated or extended by a model.
