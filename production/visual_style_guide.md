# Visual style guide — RETURN TO THE SLOPE

**Status:** Approved baseline; refine only in ways consistent with the approved REF-ARC-01 exterior and REF-CHAR-01 skier references.

## Image language

Photorealistic premium international sports advertising. Human performance observed with restraint: purposeful camera movement, believable anatomy and technique, tactile materials, natural depth of field, subtle grain, and a sophisticated, controlled grade. Favor composed, legible images over spectacle. No artificial CGI sheen, excessive HDR, fantasy resort architecture, oversaturated color, or fabricated interface text/logos.

## Palette and light

- **Outdoor snow / opening and return:** snow white, deep alpine blue, restrained natural sunrise/sunlight warmth. Cold, clear ambient fill; believable directional sun and snow bounce.
- **Facility interiors:** dark natural or charred timber, charcoal steel, stone, glazing, neutral concrete, warm amber practicals. Keep skin tones natural and contrast controlled.
- **Accent:** sparing energetic orange, principally on the athlete's kit or small wayfinding detail, never as a broad color wash.
- Maintain a readable progression from cold exposure/uncertainty to warmer human support, then natural sunlight and open space on return.

## Camera and movement

Establish geography with a restrained aerial/wide; use medium coverage for human connection and assessment; close details for tactile effort and focus; smooth tracking for skiing; stable, measured framing for clinical work. Slow motion is selective and motivated (snow detail, a safe fall beat, or a return turn), not a default effect. Avoid impossible camera paths and action that would require unsafe clinical practice.

## Architecture

A distinctive but buildable Altai sports facility: stone base, dark timber/charred wood, vertical slats, charcoal steel, panoramic glazing, functional entrance, credible spans and rooflines, snow loads implied responsibly, terrace guardrails, warm interior glow, and authentic winter context. Exterior and interiors must read as one building. Avoid chalet shorthand, excessive cantilevers, unsupported decks, impossible geometry, and random signage.

## Performance and medical credibility

Natural gait and joint loading; controlled, coached movements; appropriate sensor placement; plausible monitor graphics added in post where possible. Clinicians dress in professional sports-medical clothing, not default white coats. Injury is non-graphic. The athlete's expression and pain are understated. Show only verified equipment, procedures, services, and staff roles. No result guarantees or misleading treatment implications.

## Finishing and typography

Target a 24 fps edit and 3840 × 2160 master where supported. Use naturalistic contrast, restrained grain, highlight detail in snow, and non-crushed interior shadows. Compose for 16:9 first, then recompose the 9:16 social cut shot by shot. Place Russian titles, legal/brand copy, and official logo in post-production using supplied/verified assets; do not rely on generated lettering.
