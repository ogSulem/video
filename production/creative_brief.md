# Creative brief — RETURN TO THE SLOPE

**Status:** Stage 1 proposal; awaiting approval  
**Project:** Premium promotional film for a sports rehabilitation center in the Altai Mountains, Russia.

## Objective

Tell a concise, emotionally engaging story of an athlete moving from freedom through injury and uncertainty, into expert support and disciplined rehabilitation, and back to the mountain with renewed confidence. Position the center as a sports-performance and rehabilitation headquarters—not a conventional hospital.

## Audience and response

Athletes and active people recovering from injury, plus their families and coaches. The intended response is trust in a credible, human, sports-focused process and optimism about a supported return to sport. Avoid guarantees, medical overclaims, or implying that a particular result is assured.

## Deliverable proposal

- Main film: approximately 75 seconds (inside the 60–80 second target; never exceed approximately 90 seconds), 16:9, cinematic 24 fps edit, target UHD 3840 × 2160 where source and finishing tools support it.
- Social cut: separately composed 9:16 version derived from approved assets; not a blind center crop.
- Plan: 20 short, purposeful finished shots in ten story beats; generate/review in narrative order only after references and prompts are approved.
- Russian titles and logo artwork should be typeset in editing/design, not generated as image text.

## Narrative and required beats

1. Altai sunrise and skier's freedom.
2. A safe, non-graphic fall and immediate uncertainty.
3. A realistic, buildable center in its mountain setting.
4. Welcoming sports-oriented reception and team.
5. Credible, consent-based biomechanical assessment.
6. Ski simulator preparation; do not depict a named make/model without a verified reference.
7. Rehabilitation/recovery montage limited to verified services and equipment.
8. Genuine psychological support and multidisciplinary care.
9. The same skier returns in the same recognizable kit and equipment.
10. Clean, restrained brand card.

## Proposed initial athlete (not approved or generated)

Male athlete, approximately 32; lean, powerful build; realistic weathered skin; grey-blue eyes; subtle eyebrow scar; dark navy technical ski kit with restrained orange details; matte black helmet, dark mirrored goggles, black gloves, and believable alpine skis/boots. This is a continuity proposal only. Confirm or revise before the Stage 3 character reference.

## Brand and claims guardrails

Working Russian name/title in brief: «Реабилитационный центр Алтай»; proposed tagline: «Возвращаем на склон.»; proposed URL: altaysportreab.ru. These are not verified as the center's official name, brand, tagline, or domain. Treat all as placeholders pending authorization and verification. Never invent logos, credentials, devices, or treatment availability.

## Success criteria

Premium, emotionally clear, visually coherent, photorealistic, medically and biomechanically plausible, respectful of injury, credible in architecture and equipment, and consistent in athlete identity, wardrobe, weather, and lighting. Preserve all ten beats without forcing the original scene durations.
