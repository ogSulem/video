## Reading the numbers

**No frozen frames.** The duplicate-frame census is zero for the whole
delivery: every one of the 1891 frames is a different image. That is the direct measurement of the
claim that the picture moves continuously — the footage is re-timed with
optical flow and the stills are interpolated between key images at 4/s,
so neither half of the film is padded with holds.

**Motion where it belongs.** Generated footage averages 12 on the per-shot motion
index; staged stills average 10. Footage should carry more
movement than a staged still, and it does — the stills move because of
parallax, the focus pull and atmosphere, not because they were shaken.

**The end card is deliberately almost still** (motion 0.24): only the staged type reveal changes, which
is the point of a brand card.

(192), a dark quiet fall (94–108), warm mid-tone interiors (76–99), warm
high-key return (152–159), black card (10). The a*/b* drift within the

## What this report does and does not cover
