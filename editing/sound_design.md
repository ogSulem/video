# Sound design — initial direction

**Status:** Stage 1 proposal; no audio generated or licensed.

- Open with spacious alpine wind and a restrained tonal bed; introduce ski edge, speed, and powder detail with realistic perspective.
- The fall briefly narrows the sound field: snow impact and breath, never a sensational injury sting or graphic sound.
- Transition indoors with softened room tone, footsteps, timber/space resonance, quiet welcoming ambience, then precise but understated treadmill/sensor and simulator detail.
- Rehab montage uses rhythmic, human-scale movement sounds; only include sounds that accurately correspond to approved devices/procedures.
- Psychological/team moments create room for breath and conversation. Return to the mountain with broader wind and ski-on-snow detail; let the final stop and VO resolve naturally.
- Music proposal: premium restrained cinematic build, organic/percussive pulse increasing with training, emotional lift at return, clean resolution before/under the restrained brand card. No imitation of a known track.
- Mix for clarity and VO intelligibility; avoid over-compression, exaggerated impacts, or falsely futuristic medical sounds. Source/licensing and mix specs to be confirmed during finishing.
