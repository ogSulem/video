# Sound design — v1 implementation and finishing notes

**Status:** The original direction was implemented in the v1 render as a deterministic, synthetic score/ambience bed. No third-party music, recorded SFX, or external audio service was used.

- The bed opens with a soft alpine-air texture and restrained tonal pad, gradually adding a quiet pulse through the recovery section and a gentle harmonic lift on the return.
- A single low, soft snow-brush/thump accent supports the implied off-screen transition around 00:08. It is designed sound, not a recorded fall or injury sound.
- The interior and rehabilitation sections use the music bed only. No invented treadmill/sensor/simulator sounds, clinical beeps, footsteps, or conversation were added.
- Broader air returns with the approved Scene 9 image. The score resolves under the Scene 10 backup card; the full approved voice-over ends before the end-card copy.
- The music is an original synthetic composition generated by `video/render_altai_commercial.py`; it does not imitate a known track. A licensed or recorded replacement score and final platform-specific mix remain optional future finishing work.
- The approved voice-over file is used unchanged. The MP4 was decoded end-to-end; decoded audio peak is approximately −0.9 dBFS, without digital clipping. Confirm loudness/true-peak requirements with the final distributor/client before public release.
