## Why reception first

Reception is the first interior audience sees after the exterior reveal and supports the transition from uncertainty into welcome and expertise. It establishes material language, window character, warm/cool balance, and human scale. This asset depicts reception only; it does not introduce assessment, treatment, simulator, pool, or other equipment zones.
