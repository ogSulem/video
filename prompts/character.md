# Stage 3 image brief — master skier character

**Status:** Proposed design only. Do not generate until Stage 2 is explicitly approved and the user approves this character concept. Stage 3 calls for exactly one image.

## Proposed appearance for review

Male athlete, approximately 32 years old; lean, powerful athletic build; realistic mountain-weathered skin; grey-blue eyes; subtle eyebrow scar. Dark navy technical ski jacket and pants with restrained orange accents; matte black helmet; dark mirrored goggles; black gloves; realistic alpine boots and skis. Premium, functional equipment rather than conspicuous luxury branding.

## Intended reference function

A clean, photorealistic identity/wardrobe reference that makes face, build, scar, colors, and ski kit legible for later continuity. Do not use this brief to make video or multiple views. Final composition, pose, and any face visibility will be set in the approved generation prompt.

## Guardrails

Natural anatomy and proportions; credible technical fabric, closures, helmet, goggles, gloves, boots, and skis; no logos or readable text; no injury depiction; no exaggerated action. Keep identity stable in injury, rehabilitation, and return scenes. No generation until the earlier approval gate and character-design approval are both complete.
