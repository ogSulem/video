# Stage 2 image brief — master exterior architecture

**Status:** Prompt brief only. Do not generate until Stage 1 is approved. When authorized, generate exactly one image and present it for review.

## Intent

Establish one distinctive, photorealistic, buildable premium sports rehabilitation center in a believable winter Altai Mountains setting. This image becomes the proposed architectural continuity anchor, subject to approval.

## Required design language

A natural stone base, dark timber or charred-wood cladding, visible vertical wooden slats, charcoal steel framing, generous but structurally plausible panoramic glazing, a functional clearly readable entrance, realistic rooflines and proportions, guarded functional terraces, snow-aware construction, and warm interior light. Authentic alpine/mountain landscape and snow; no chalet cliché. Composition should show the building as an actual facility, not a fantasy resort.

## Image quality / composition

Photorealistic premium architectural-commercial still, 16:9 landscape framing with the full facility and useful context visible, eye-level to modestly elevated cinematic establishing viewpoint, natural winter daylight with restrained warm interior glow. Materials must read as real stone, wood, steel, glass, and snow. Plausible scale, structural spans, entrances, access, and guardrails. No excessive cantilever, impossible geometry, oversized roof gestures, or CGI sheen.

## Exclusions

No people required; no text, signage, logos, medical claims, invented brand marks, or watermarks. Do not depict equipment/services that imply unverified offerings. Do not generate alternate views or a collage in this stage.

## Review checklist

Photorealism; Altai winter plausibility; buildable structure and access; coherent snow/roof/terrace treatment; material realism; warm/cold light balance; distinctive but credible identity; clean image without text/artifacts. Await explicit user approval before using as the master or advancing to the skier image.
